CRITICAL ACTION REQUIRED FOR GO-LIVE:

Android Play Integrity (Firebase App Check) will BLOCK your production app users if you do not add your Google Play Console SHA-256 certificate footprint to the Firebase Console. 

Follow these exact steps before releasing to the Play Store:
1. Go to your Google Play Console -> CRADI Mobile app.
2. In the left menu, navigate to Release -> Setup -> App Integrity.
3. Click on the "App Signing" tab.
4. Scroll down to "App signing key certificate" and copy the "SHA-256 certificate fingerprint".
5. Go to your Firebase Console -> Project Settings -> App Check.
6. Click on your Android App, then under 'Play Integrity', click 'Edit' or 'Add'.
7. Paste the copied SHA-256 fingerprint and Save.

Note: Local debug builds or internal testing APKs may use a different SHA-256 (e.g., from `~/.android/debug.keystore`). The production one described above is REQUIRED for users downloading from the Google Play Store.
